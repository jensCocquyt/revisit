Meterbill pricing

Simple, usage-based pricing. Prices on this page are current as of August 2026; we adjust tiers roughly twice a year and always announce changes thirty days in advance to existing customers. Grandfathering applies for twelve months after any price change.

Developer — free

Up to 50,000 metered events per month, one project, community support, seven-day data retention. No credit card required. Rate limited to 20 requests per second.

Team — 99 euros per month

One million metered events included, then 0.08 euros per additional thousand. Five projects, ninety-day retention, email support with a two-business-day response target, webhook delivery with retries, and SSO via Google and Microsoft.

Scale — 499 euros per month

Ten million events included, then 0.05 euros per additional thousand. Unlimited projects, thirteen-month retention, priority support with a four-hour response target during business hours, SAML SSO, audit logs, and a 99.9 percent uptime commitment with service credits.

Enterprise — talk to us

Custom event volume, custom retention, dedicated tenancy options, a named support contact, and security review support. Annual invoicing available in EUR, USD, and GBP.

All paid plans are month-to-month with no minimum term; annual prepay takes ten percent off. VAT is added where applicable. The fair-use policy and the definition of a metered event are in the pricing FAQ below.
