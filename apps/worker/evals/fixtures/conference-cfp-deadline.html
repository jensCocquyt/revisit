DataInfra Summit 2026 — Call for Proposals

DataInfra Summit returns to Rotterdam on 14–15 October 2026, and the call for proposals is now open. We are looking for talks from practitioners who run data systems in production: storage engines, stream processing, orchestration, reliability, and the operational war stories that never make it into vendor keynotes.

We accept three formats: thirty-minute talks, five-minute lightning talks, and ninety-minute hands-on workshops. First-time speakers are explicitly welcome — the program committee offers a free abstract review round and a rehearsal slot with an experienced speaker if you want one. Talks are selected on the substance of the abstract, not the fame of the speaker; we anonymize the first review pass.

The submission deadline is 30 September 2026, 23:59 CET. Notifications go out in the second week of November, and the final program is published on 1 December. Accepted speakers receive a full conference pass, and we cover travel up to 400 euros for speakers without employer support.

What makes a strong submission? Concrete numbers, honest failure analysis, and a clear takeaway the audience can apply. Skip the product pitch — sponsored slots exist for that and the audience can smell the difference. If your talk includes a demo, tell us what happens when the demo gods are unkind; a recorded fallback is fine.

Submit through the portal linked below. One proposal per form; multiple submissions are allowed but we rarely accept two talks from the same speaker.
