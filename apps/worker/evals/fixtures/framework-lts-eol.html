Angular v17 support schedule and end of life

Angular releases follow a predictable support cadence: six months of active support, during which regularly-scheduled updates and patches ship, followed by twelve months of long-term support during which only critical fixes and security patches land.

Long-term support for Angular v17 ends on 31 May 2027. After that date v17 receives no further security patches, and applications still on it should be treated as running unsupported software.

Teams planning their upgrade path should target v18 or later well before the cutoff. The update guide covers the mechanical steps; for most applications the migration is a matter of hours, not days. Material and CDK follow the same schedule as the framework.

If you maintain a library, note that supporting unsupported framework majors carries real cost: your dependency ranges keep old peer versions alive in the ecosystem long after the framework team has moved on.
