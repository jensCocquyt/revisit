Open Infrastructure Fund — autumn 2026 grant round

The Open Infrastructure Fund supports maintainers of open source software that the internet quietly depends on: build tools, protocol implementations, cryptographic libraries, package registries, and the unglamorous glue in between. The autumn 2026 round distributes 1.2 million euros in grants between 10,000 and 80,000 euros per project.

Applications open 1 September and close 15 October 2026 at 18:00 UTC. Decisions are announced on 8 December; funds are disbursed in January. Late applications are not considered under any circumstances — the review committee's schedule is the constraint, not the tooling.

What we fund: maintainer time for security work, dependency and toolchain modernization, documentation overhauls, governance formation, and succession planning for single-maintainer projects. What we do not fund: feature development driven by a single company's roadmap, marketing, conference travel, or projects less than two years old.

The application is deliberately short: a two-page proposal, a budget, evidence of adoption (reverse-dependency counts, download statistics, or downstream testimonials), and a sustainability statement. Past recipients report four to six hours of total effort. Reviewers are practicing maintainers and every application receives written feedback, funded or not.

Projects previously declined are welcome to reapply; roughly a quarter of this spring's grants went to second-time applicants. Questions reach the program committee via the address below until one week before the deadline.
