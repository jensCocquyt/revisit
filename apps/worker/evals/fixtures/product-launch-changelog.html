Ledgerline Changelog

All notable changes to the Ledgerline platform, newest first. We ship weekly; this page is updated every Thursday. Entries tagged beta are behind a feature flag and may change without notice.

Week of 10 August 2026

Multi-currency invoices are now generally available. Invoices can carry line items in any of 32 currencies with the settlement currency chosen at issue time; historical rates come from the ECB daily fixing. The reconciliation report gained a currency-exposure column.

Week of 3 August 2026

Beta: webhook delivery now supports ordered mode, guaranteeing per-account ordering at the cost of throughput. Opt in per endpoint in the developer console. We also raised the API rate limit for read endpoints from 100 to 300 requests per minute on all paid plans.

Week of 27 July 2026

The CSV export format for statements changed: two new columns (settlement_id, fx_rate) were appended. Parsers that select columns by position must be updated; parsers selecting by header name are unaffected. This was announced four weeks in advance on the developer mailing list.

Week of 20 July 2026

Fixed a rounding inconsistency in prorated refunds smaller than one cent. Amounts now always round half-to-even in the customer's favor. No API surface changed.
