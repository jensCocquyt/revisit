Five weeknight curries that respect your Tuesday

A weeknight curry has one job: deliver something deeply flavored in the time it takes the rice cooker to finish. These five earn a permanent rotation slot in our kitchen, and none of them needs more than one pot, thirty-five minutes, or a shopping trip beyond a decently stocked supermarket.

The red lentil dal is the fastest of the set. Lentils, coconut milk, and a tarka of mustard seeds, garlic, and curry leaves bloomed in hot oil at the end — the tarka is not optional, it is the entire point. Twenty-two minutes, and it reheats better than it serves fresh, so double it.

The chickpea and spinach curry leans on the tinned chickpea, rightly. Onions cooked harder than feels polite, tomato paste toasted until brick-colored, then garam masala, chickpeas with half their liquid, and a mountain of spinach folded in at the end. Serve with yogurt and something crunchy.

For the Thai-style green curry, the jarred paste is fine — bloom it in the thick top of the coconut milk until the oil splits, and it will taste like you made it. Green beans and tofu, or chicken thighs if that is your household politics. Fish sauce at the end, a squeeze of lime, done in under thirty.
